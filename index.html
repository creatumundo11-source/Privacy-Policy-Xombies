Xombies · Meta Quest

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what user data the virtual reality game Xombies ("the app", "we") collects and processes when you play it on Meta Quest, how that data is used, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you can ask us to delete it.

What we useYour Meta user ID and username, plus your Endless scores and achievements.
What forOnline leaderboards and achievements. No ads, no analytics.
Your controlEmail us anytime to delete your data. We answer within 30 days.

1Data the app collects and how it is collected

Xombies uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK. Through it, the app collects and processes the following data from your Meta account while you play:

DataHow it is collectedWhat it is used for
User ID (your Meta account's unique identifier for this app) Provided automatically by the Meta Horizon Platform SDK when the app uses online features (leaderboards and achievements).
  • To save your best Endless-mode result on the online leaderboards under your account.
  • To record your achievement unlocks and progress on your account.
User profile: your Meta username / display name Provided by the Meta Horizon Platform SDK together with the leaderboard entries. To show the names of the players on the "Top Survivors" leaderboard inside the game (including your own name next to your result).
Game results: waves survived and score in Endless mode, difficulty, achievements earned Sent by the app to Meta's leaderboard and achievement services when you finish an Endless run or unlock an achievement. To rank players on the leaderboards and to show your achievements on your Meta profile.

We do not collect your real name, email address, location, contacts, voice, camera images, hand or body tracking data, or payment information, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

2Where the data is stored and who can see it

  • Leaderboards and achievements are stored by Meta on its servers, linked to your Meta account. Your username and your best result on each leaderboard are visible to other Xombies players. Your achievements are visible according to your Meta privacy settings.
  • We do not run our own servers and do not copy, sell, rent or share this data with anyone. As the developer we can only see the same leaderboard entries that any player can see, through Meta's developer tools.
  • Meta's own processing of your account data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

3Data stored only on your headset

The game also saves a small amount of data only on your headset so it works the way you left it: your settings (language, volume, turning style, comfort vignette, graphics), your progress (tutorial completed, unlocked levels, difficulty) and your personal Endless records. This data never leaves your device.

4How long the data is kept

  • Leaderboard entries and achievements are kept while the game's leaderboards and achievements exist, or until you ask us to delete them (see section 5).
  • Data on your headset is kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

5How to request deletion of your data

You can ask us at any time to delete the data associated with your account in Xombies:

  1. Send an email to creatumundo11@gmail.com with the subject "Xombies data deletion request".
  2. Include the Meta username you use in the game, so we can find your entries.
  3. Within 30 days we will delete your entries from the Xombies leaderboards, remove your Xombies achievement progress where Meta's developer tools allow it (otherwise we will tell you how to request it from Meta), and confirm by email.

To delete the data stored on your headset, clear the app's data or uninstall Xombies. You can also delete your whole Meta account and its data from your Meta account settings, as described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

6Children

The game is intended for players old enough to use Meta Quest under Meta's terms and is rated for mature audiences because of its violence. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data as described in section 5.

7Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects the data it collects (for example, new online features), we will update this page and its effective date before that change is released.
