XOMBIES – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the virtual reality game Xombies ("the game", "we") handles when you play it on Meta Quest. In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no online features operated by us.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of data only on your headset so it works the way you left it:

  • Your settings (for example volume, turning style, comfort vignette and graphics options).
  • Your progress (for example whether you completed the tutorial and which levels are unlocked).

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Meta platform services

When the game starts, it uses the Meta Horizon platform to verify that you own a legitimate copy (an "entitlement check"). This check is performed by Meta; we do not receive your personal information from it. Meta's handling of your data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is intended for players old enough to use Meta Quest under Meta's terms and is rated for mature audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding online features), we will update this page and its effective date before that change is released.
